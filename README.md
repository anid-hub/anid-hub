## Scientific (Laboratory) Competency
* **Spatial-Temporal Trajectory Modeling:** Developed and validated mathematical models for vessel kinematics using Kalman filtering and spline interpolation on irregular time-series data.
* **Maritime Anomaly Detection:** Engineered statistical pipelines and unsupervised machine learning models (e.g., ST-DBSCAN) to systematically isolate telemetry spoofing, loitering, and route deviations.
* **Data Integrity & QA/QC Frameworks:** Designed automated validation protocols to filter, clean, and harmonize multi-gigabyte, noisy, and corrupt raw AIS datasets.
* **Geospatial Experimental Design:** Built computational frameworks for spatial predicate evaluation, geofencing, and point-in-polygon analysis applied to Marine Protected Areas (MPAs) and shipping corridors.
* **Reproducible Computational Research:** Established standardized, containerized environments to ensure full auditability, reproducibility, and peer-verification of data-driven scientific findings.
